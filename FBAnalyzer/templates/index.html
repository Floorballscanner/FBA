Analyse your team performance

Floorball Scanner gives you information about your team performance. With the software you are able to see how your team and each line performs on the field — turning game footage into decisions you can act on before the next shift.

Real-time data, live

With Floorball Scanner you have access to real-time data of your team during the game.

  • Ball possession
  • Time on court and ball with averages
  • Saved, missed, and blocked shots with shot map
  • Goals and plus/minus
  • Expected Goals (xG)

Shot map

Map easily your and opponents' shots and get a picture of how shots are distributed on the field.

Advanced statistics

See deeper into the game result with advanced statistics, such as expected goals (xG) and ball possession of each line individually. With advanced statistics provided by Floorball Scanner you are able to make better and more informed decisions also during the game.

Intuitive visualizations

Sophisticated graphs and pictures help you form a better understanding of how your team and lines are doing against the opponent.

F-Liiga live results & expected goals

Follow every F-Liiga match live with expected goals (xG) you won't find anywhere else — by player, by line, and by team, plus win probability and shot maps. Available as its own license, or included in every Team and Club license.
